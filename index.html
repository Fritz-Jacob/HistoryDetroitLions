The History of the Detroit Lions

This is a page all about the Detroit Lions and the players I like!

The Detroit Lions are a professional football team based in Detroit, Michigan. The team has a long history in professional football and has built a passionate fan base over the years.

Lions Facts

  • The team is based in Detroit, Michigan.
  • The Lions play their home games at Ford Field.
  • The team's colors include Honolulu blue and silver.

Team History

  • The franchise began as the Portsmouth Spartans before moving to Detroit in 1934 and becoming the Detroit Lions.
  • The team won multiple NFL championships during its early history.
